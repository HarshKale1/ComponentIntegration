Turn any financial news into an interactive market impact graph.

FinFlow AI analyzes news events and geopolitical developments, identifies the entities involved, and builds a connected picture of how the shock could propagate across commodities, countries, sectors, and financial assets. Every relationship is scored, evidence-labeled, and explained.
